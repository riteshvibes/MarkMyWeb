# IDENTRA

### Know. Prove. Protect.

> **Your digital identity, under your control.**

Identra is a privacy-first digital identity intelligence platform that helps users **discover, verify, understand, and protect their identity across the public web**.

Instead of simply scanning the internet and generating a static privacy report, Identra creates a **living digital identity map** that connects online profiles, websites, personal information, relationships, and public signals.

It combines **cryptographic identity claims, identity graphs, ML-based risk analysis, inference detection, shadow discovery, and privacy simulations** into one platform.

---

## ✨ Why Identra?

Your digital identity isn't stored in one place.

It is fragmented across:

- Personal websites
- GitHub
- LinkedIn
- Portfolios
- Blogs
- Social profiles
- Project pages
- Public posts
- Images
- Events
- Organizations
- Public identifiers

Individually, these signals may seem harmless.

The real risk comes from **connecting them together**.
